FAQ

Good questions.

The things people ask before they book. Don't see yours? Reach out and we'll answer it.

Are you licensed to fly drones commercially?

Yes. Every flight is run by an FAA Part 107 certified commercial drone pilot. We handle airspace authorizations and location permits as part of planning.

What kinds of drone jobs do you take?

Events and shows, holiday light displays, real estate, hotels and venues, farms, construction progress, roof and building inspections, industrial inspections, mapping, film production support and more. See Drone Services for the full list. If a drone can do it legally, ask.

Do you do inspections?

Yes. We fly roofs, facades, towers, turbines, solar arrays and other hard-to-reach structures and deliver organized photo sets and video. Our pilot has flown industrial wind turbine inspections across the US.

Do you really shoot underwater?

Yes. From pools to open water, we shoot below the surface, including over / under shots that split the frame between air and water.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Southern California and shoot across Orange County, Los Angeles, San Diego and the coast. Further out? Ask us.

What do I actually get at the end?

Edited, ready-to-post videos in the formats you need: 16:9 for your website and YouTube, 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, plus 4:5 and 1:1 cuts when they're useful. We confirm the exact list before the shoot.

Can you make content for us every month?

Yes. Our Monthly Content Partner plan includes a half-day shoot every month, 12 to 16 edited vertical videos, planning and hooks, drone footage wherever it's legal, one round of revisions and a monthly performance review.

Can you run our social media?

Yes. We can handle planning, filming, editing, captions and posting on Instagram and TikTok, or just the pieces you need. See Social Media & UGC.

What is UGC?

User-generated content: videos filmed like a real customer or creator talking about your product or place. It performs well as ads and on product pages because it doesn't look like a commercial.

Can you feature our business on your channels?

Yes. We do sponsored reels, venue and product features, partnership ads and giveaways on our own Instagram and TikTok. Ask for our media kit on the Creator Collabs page.

How much does it cost?

One-off projects are quoted based on locations, which zones we shoot (air, ground, water) and what you need delivered. Monthly plans have a set monthly rate. Tell us about your project and we'll send a quote.

Can we book just a drone pilot for our event or production?

Yes. We do drone-only bookings for venues, event companies, agencies and other production crews.

How do revisions work?

Monthly plans include one round of revisions. For project work we agree on revisions in the quote, before anything is shot.

Why does it matter that you run your own channels?

Because we're tested by the same algorithm every day. The hooks, pacing and formats we use for clients are the ones currently working on our own Instagram and TikTok, which each have 11k+ followers.
